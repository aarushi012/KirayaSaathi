# Delhi Tenancy & Rental Legal Framework

## Statutory Overview
- **Primary Legislation**: Delhi Rent Control Act, 1958 & Model Tenancy Act (MTA) Guidelines adopted for the National Capital Territory (NCT) of Delhi.
- **Enforcement Authority**: Rent Controller Courts / Revenue Authorities, Government of NCT of Delhi.
- **Police Jurisdiction**: Mandatory tenant identity verification via Delhi Police Online Tenant Verification Portal.

---

## 1. Security Deposit Regulations & Benchmarks
- **Standard Ceiling**: 1 to 2 months' rent for residential properties.
- **Maximum Legal Benchmark**: Any security deposit demand exceeding 2 months' rent is considered non-standard and should be negotiated downwards.
- **Statutory Refund Timeline**: Landlords are required to refund the security deposit within 30 days of the tenant peacefully handing over vacant possession.
- **Allowed Deductions**: Deductions are restricted strictly to unpaid utility bills, unpaid rent, and documented physical damage beyond normal wear and tear.
- **Forfeiture Rules**: Blanket forfeiture of the entire deposit upon early lease termination is legally contested; landlords may only claim actual provable rental loss while seeking replacement tenants.

---

## 2. Mandatory Notice Periods & Eviction Protections
- **Notice to Vacate (Standard)**: Minimum 30 days (1 month) written notice given by either party.
- **Notice for Periodic Tenancies**: At least 15 days notice before the end of the monthly tenancy cycle.
- **Illegal / Summary Eviction**: Landlords cannot forcibly disconnect electricity, water, or lock premises without a formal decree from the Competent Authority.
- **Breach Notice**: In case of alleged non-payment or property misuse, tenant must be given at least 15 days written notice to rectify before eviction proceedings.

---

## 3. Lock-in Period Standards
- **Standard Duration**: 1 to 3 months in typical 11-month lease agreements.
- **Long Lock-in Clauses**: Lock-in periods exceeding 6 months in residential leases are considered high-friction and restrictive for tenants.
- **Penalty on Breach**: Liquidated damages during lock-in cannot exceed actual lost rent until a new tenant occupies the premises.

---

## 4. Maintenance & Repair Allocation
- **Landlord Responsibilities**:
  - Structural integrity, roof leaks, foundational dampness, exterior walls.
  - Major electrical rewiring, main distribution board, sewer line blockages.
  - Pre-existing water heater or structural plumbing failures.
- **Tenant Responsibilities**:
  - Minor day-to-day maintenance: light bulbs, tap washers, minor drain cleaning, internal cleaning.
  - Maximum standard tenant cap for single minor repair: ₹500 to ₹1,000 per incident.
- **Unfair Clauses**: Clauses stating "Tenant is responsible for all repairs regardless of origin or pre-existing defects" are inequitable.

---

## 5. Landlord Access & Quiet Enjoyment
- **Advance Notice Requirement**: Landlord must provide at least 24 hours prior written or electronic notice before entering the premises.
- **Permitted Entry Hours**: Entry is permitted only between 7:00 AM and 8:00 PM for legitimate inspection, repair, or prospective buyer/tenant viewings.
- **Unannounced Inspections**: Arbitrary or unannounced landlord visits without verifiable emergency violate the tenant's right to quiet enjoyment.

---

## 6. Rent Escalation & Revision
- **Standard Benchmark**: 5% to 10% annual escalation upon lease renewal (10% is customary for residential premises in Delhi NCR).
- **Mid-term Hikes**: Rent cannot be increased unilaterally during the subsistence of the agreed lease term without explicit prior written agreement.

---

## 7. Move-out Repainting & Turnover Deductions
- **Standard Practice**: Landlords are responsible for periodic repainting and normal aging/fading of walls.
- **Deduction Criteria**: Deducting automatic 1-month rent or mandatory fixed repainting charges without evidence of unauthorized wall modifications (deep holes, major stains) is non-standard.

---

## 8. Common Predatory Clauses & Negotiation Scripts

### Predatory Clause: Unannounced Landlord Entry
> *"Landlord may enter premises at any time without notice for inspection."*
- **Tenant Counter**: *"Could we update this clause to specify at least 24 hours prior notice before non-emergency visits during reasonable daytime hours?"*

### Predatory Clause: Automatic Deposit Repainting Forfeiture
> *"1 month rent shall be automatically deducted for repainting and cleaning upon checkout."*
- **Tenant Counter**: *"Could we agree that deductions will only apply to actual documented damage beyond normal wear and tear, based on itemized contractor invoices?"*
