# Bangalore (Bengaluru) Tenancy & Rental Legal Framework

## Statutory Overview
- **Primary Legislation**: Karnataka Rent Act, 1999 & Karnataka Model Tenancy Guidelines (Urban Development Dept).
- **Enforcement Authority**: Rent Court / Small Causes Court / Assistant Commissioner, Government of Karnataka.
- **Police Jurisdiction**: Mandatory tenant registration with Bengaluru City Police (Seva Sindhu / Police Verification portal).

---

## 1. Security Deposit Regulations & Bangalore Customary Practices
- **Historical Customary Practice**: Historically 10 months' rent demanded by landlords in Bengaluru (Indiranagar, Koramangala, Whitefield, HSR Layout, Bellandur).
- **Model Tenancy Act (MTA) Benchmark**: Caps residential security deposits at a maximum of **2 months' rent**.
- **Market Negotiation Guideline**: Tenants should strongly negotiate deposits down to **2 to 3 months' rent** (maximum 4-5 months in high-demand micro-markets).
- **Statutory Refund Timeline**: Refund must be credited within 30 days of vacating with key handover.
- **Deposit Escrow & Interest**: Landlord holding massive 10-month deposits must account for every deduction with physical invoices and photos.

---

## 2. The Infamous "1-Month Painting Deduction" Clause
- **Common Predatory Term**: Landlords in Bangalore frequently insert an automatic clause: *"1 month rent will be deducted from security deposit for house painting upon exit."*
- **Legal Reality**:
  - The Karnataka Rent Act and Indian Contract Act distinguish between **normal wear and tear** (fading of paint due to sunlight, time) vs **negligent tenant damage** (crayon marks, physical wall chipping, unauthorized wall drilling).
  - Normal aging paint is an owner's capital maintenance cost.
  - Automatic deduction of ₹30,000–₹60,000 without actual repainting or contractor invoice is legally challengeable.
- **Negotiation Solution**: Replace automatic fixed 1-month deduction with: *"Tenant shall either repaint through an approved painter before leaving, or pay actual contractor painting costs only if walls show damage beyond normal wear and tear."*

---

## 3. Mandatory Notice Periods & Eviction Safeguards
- **Standard Notice to Vacate**: 1 to 2 months (30 to 60 days) written notice.
- **Notice on Rent Escalation**: Minimum 90 days notice before term expiry if landlord intends to revise rent.
- **Unlawful Eviction Protections**: Section 27 of Karnataka Rent Act prohibits landlords from disconnecting water (BWSSB), electricity (BESCOM), or elevator access to force eviction.

---

## 4. Maintenance & Repair Distribution
- **Landlord Responsibility**:
  - Structural seepage, wall dampness, bathroom waterproofing, exterior paint.
  - BESCOM main electrical panel, BWSSB water pump & sump motor replacements.
  - Building clubhouse and apartment corpus fund contributions.
- **Tenant Responsibility**:
  - Daily internal maintenance, consumable fixtures, water filter replacements.
  - Minor repair cost cap: ₹500 to ₹1,000 per occurrence.

---

## 5. Landlord Access & Quiet Enjoyment
- **Advance Notice Requirement**: Minimum 24 hours prior written notice before visiting.
- **Permitted Entry Hours**: 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM.
- **Unannounced Entry / Broker Walk-ins**: Landlords cannot send prospective tenants or brokers without prior scheduling and tenant confirmation.

---

## 6. Rent Escalation & Revision
- **Standard Benchmark**: 5% to 8% annual escalation (5% standard; 8% to 10% in prime IT corridors).
- **Automatic Renewal**: Must be mutually executed on fresh e-stamp paper (₹100 to ₹500 Karnataka e-Stamp).

---

## 7. Common Predatory Clauses & Negotiation Scripts

### Predatory Clause: Automatic 1-Month Painting Deduction
> *"Landlord shall deduct 1 full month rent from deposit towards painting and cleaning charges upon vacating regardless of tenure length."*
- **Tenant Counter**: *"Could we agree that deductions will only reflect actual painting expense against GST invoice if the walls show damage beyond normal wear and tear?"*

### Predatory Clause: 10-Month Upfront Deposit
> *"Tenant shall pay 10 months rent as refundable security deposit."*
- **Tenant Counter**: *"Under the Model Tenancy framework, standard residential deposits are 2 to 3 months. Could we settle on 3 months' deposit given my verified corporate employment credentials?"*
