# Chandigarh (UT) Tenancy & Rental Legal Framework

## Statutory Overview
- **Primary Legislation**: East Punjab Urban Rent Restriction (Extension to Chandigarh) Act, 1952 & Chandigarh Administration Tenancy Bylaws.
- **Enforcement Authority**: Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) / Rent Controller, Chandigarh Administration.
- **Police Jurisdiction**: Mandatory tenant registration with Chandigarh Police (E-Saathi / Beat Officer Verification).

---

## 1. Security Deposit Regulations & Benchmarks
- **Standard Ceiling**: 1 to 2 months' rent for residential flats, marla houses, and floors across Sectors 1 to 63.
- **Maximum Legal Benchmark**: Deposits exceeding 2 months are non-standard in Chandigarh Union Territory.
- **Statutory Refund Timeline**: Refund within 30 days upon key handover and meter reading settlement.
- **Permissible Deductions**: Verified unpaid electricity bills (under Chandigarh Electricity Dept), water meter dues, and verified damage.
- **Strict Prohibition**: Mandatory non-refundable turnover or deep-cleaning fees cannot be arbitrarily deducted without itemized contractor proofs.

---

## 2. Notice Periods & UT Municipal Regulations
- **Standard Notice to Vacate**: Minimum 30 days (1 month) written notice by certified post or registered email/WhatsApp.
- **Sub-Metering & Utilities**: Chandigarh Administration mandates separate sub-meters for floors; landlords cannot charge inflated commercial electricity rates on domestic lines.
- **Eviction Procedures**: Summary self-help eviction (changing locks, dumping tenant belongings) is a cognizable offense under UT Police bylaws.

---

## 3. Floor Sharing & Sub-letting Regulations
- **Paying Guest (PG) & Sharing Rules**: Chandigarh Administration has strict registered PG guidelines regarding minimum square footage per occupant and fire safety.
- **Sub-letting**: Standard residential leases strictly prohibit third-party sub-letting without notarized landlord consent.

---

## 4. Maintenance & Repair Distribution
- **Landlord Responsibility**:
  - Terrace water-proofing, rainwater pipe maintenance, seepage between floors.
  - Overhead water tank structural integrity and main electrical feeder lines.
  - Estate Office building code compliance and authorized construction maintenance.
- **Tenant Responsibility**:
  - Daily internal maintenance, interior electrical fixtures, tap washers.
  - Normal repair cap for tenant: ₹500 to ₹1,000.

---

## 5. Landlord Access & Verification Rules
- **Advance Notice**: 24 hours prior written notice before entry.
- **Allowed Hours**: 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM on working days or mutually agreed weekends.
- **Police Verification**: Both landlord and tenant share the legal obligation to submit the tenant verification form within 15 days of occupancy.

---

## 6. Rent Escalation & Revision
- **Standard Benchmark**: 5% to 7% annual escalation upon lease renewal (7% standard in UT residential sectors).
- **Revision Mechanism**: Written notice at least 30 days before annual expiration before implementing revised rent.

---

## 7. Common Predatory Clauses & Negotiation Scripts

### Predatory Clause: Inflated Electricity Surcharge
> *"Tenant shall pay a fixed flat charge of ₹12 per unit for electricity regardless of actual slab bill."*
- **Tenant Counter**: *"Could we agree that electricity will be charged strictly as per the official Chandigarh Electricity Department domestic tariff and actual sub-meter consumption?"*

### Predatory Clause: Instant Lockout for Minor Delay
> *"Failure to pay rent by 5th of month allows landlord to immediately lock premises."*
- **Tenant Counter**: *"Could we include a standard 7-day grace period with reasonable late fee before any formal legal notice is issued?"*
