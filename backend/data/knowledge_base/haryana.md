# Haryana Tenancy & Rental Legal Framework

## Statutory Overview
- **Primary Legislation**: Haryana Urban (Control of Rent and Eviction) Act, 1973 & Haryana Model Tenancy Guidelines for Gurugram, Faridabad, Panchkula, and Sonipat.
- **Enforcement Authority**: Rent Control Authority / District Revenue Courts, Government of Haryana.
- **Police Jurisdiction**: Mandatory tenant police verification via Haryana Police HarSamay Portal or Resident Welfare Association (RWA) security desk.

---

## 1. Security Deposit Regulations & Benchmarks
- **Standard Ceiling**: 1 to 2 months' rent for residential apartments in Gurugram, Faridabad, and Panchkula.
- **Maximum Legal Benchmark**: Any demand exceeding 2 months' rent is flagged as non-standard under the Model Tenancy Act principles.
- **Statutory Refund Timeline**: Refund within 30 days upon key handover and utility clearance.
- **Gated Community / High-rise Norms**: Gated society move-out deposit deductions must be backed by society management clearance (NOC) and itemized repairs.
- **Interest Protection**: Withholding deposit beyond 30 days without justifiable reason incurs statutory interest liabilities.

---

## 2. Notice Periods & High-Rise Society Rules
- **Standard Notice to Vacate**: Minimum 30 days (1 month) written notice by either party.
- **Society Move-in/Move-out NOC**: Landlord is responsible for clearing all historical builder/RWA maintenance arrears prior to tenant move-in.
- **Move-out Gate Pass Charges**: High-rise societies often charge move-in/move-out lift usage fees; the agreement must clearly state who bears these administrative charges.

---

## 3. Maintenance Split in Gated Societies (Gurugram / Faridabad)
- **RWA Maintenance Split**:
  - Regular Monthly Society Maintenance (lifts, security, clubhouse, cleaning): Tenant responsibility (if included or agreed).
  - Sinking Fund, Capital Improvement Levies, Generator Replacement Funds: 100% Landlord responsibility.
- **Internal Appliance Maintenance**:
  - Landlord: Air conditioning gas leaks due to aged coil, built-in chimney motor replacement, geyser tank corrosion.
  - Tenant: Filter cleaning, routine AC seasonal servicing, light bulb replacement.

---

## 4. Lock-in Period & Early Exit
- **Standard Benchmark**: 1 to 3 months lock-in period.
- **Corporate Relocation Clause**: For IT/corporate professionals in Gurugram/Faridabad, a standard diploma/transfer exit clause allows lease termination with 30-day notice without penalty upon job transfer.
- **Liquidated Damages**: Capped at actual rent until new occupancy, never exceeding deposit amount.

---

## 5. Landlord Access & Privacy
- **Advance Notice**: Minimum 24 hours prior written notice before entry.
- **Permitted Entry Hours**: 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM.
- **Unannounced Entry**: Landlords cannot enter unannounced or grant third-party brokers access without tenant presence or consent.

---

## 6. Rent Escalation & Revision
- **Standard Benchmark**: 5% to 10% annual escalation upon lease renewal (10% standard in DLF, Cyber City, Golf Course Road, and Sohna Road sectors).
- **Advance Written Notice**: 60 days prior notice required before lease expiry to negotiate renewal terms.

---

## 7. Common Predatory Clauses & Negotiation Scripts

### Predatory Clause: Tenant Bears Society Capital Levies
> *"Tenant shall pay all society maintenance invoices including special sinking fund levies and asset replacement fees."*
- **Tenant Counter**: *"Could we specify that tenant pays only routine monthly operational maintenance, while any capital replacement or sinking fund charges remain the owner's responsibility?"*

### Predatory Clause: Blanket Move-out Painting Forfeiture
> *"1 month rent shall be deducted from the security deposit upon vacating for repainting."*
- **Tenant Counter**: *"Could we agree that repainting deductions only apply if walls have deep structural damage beyond normal fading, based on contractor bills?"*
