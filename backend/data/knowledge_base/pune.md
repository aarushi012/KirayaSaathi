# Pune (Maharashtra) Tenancy & Rental Legal Framework

## Statutory Overview
- **Primary Legislation**: Maharashtra Rent Control Act, 1999 (MRC Act) & Chapter VII (Section 24) on Leave and License Agreements.
- **Enforcement Authority**: Competent Authority (Rent Control Act) / Divisional Commissioner, Government of Maharashtra.
- **Police Jurisdiction**: Mandatory tenant police verification (e-KYC / Tenant Information form submission on Pune City Police Portal).

---

## 1. Security Deposit Regulations & Market Norms
- **Standard Ceiling**: 2 to 3 months' rent across Pune (Kothrud, Baner, Wakad, Hinjewadi, Viman Nagar, Kharadi, Hadapsar).
- **Maximum Legal Benchmark**: Deposits exceeding 3 months' rent in residential Leave & License agreements are contrary to Model Tenancy benchmarks.
- **Statutory Refund Timeline**: Refund within 30 days upon key handover and electricity/gas meter reading settlement.
- **Permissible Deductions**: Unpaid MSEDCL electricity bills, piped PNG gas dues, society maintenance arrears, and proven physical damage.

---

## 2. Leave and License Agreement Framework
- **Mandatory E-Registration**: Under Section 55 of the Maharashtra Rent Control Act, **all Leave and License agreements must be in writing and registered** with the Department of Registration and Stamps (e-Registration / IGR Maharashtra).
- **Legal Status of Tenant**: Under a registered Leave and License agreement, the occupier is legally a "Licensee" (not a tenant with tenancy rights), providing balanced rights to both Licensor and Licensee.
- **Eviction Relief (Section 24)**: Licensors have a summary statutory right to repossess premises upon expiry of license period, and Licensees are protected from illegal mid-term dispossession.

---

## 3. Mandatory Notice Periods & Lock-in Terms
- **Standard Notice to Vacate**: 1 month (30 days) written notice by either party.
- **Standard Lock-in**: 1 to 3 months.
- **IT Corridor Job Transfer Clause**: Standard exit clause allows Hinjewadi / Magarpatta IT professionals to terminate with 30-day notice without penalty in case of company job transfer or relocation.

---

## 4. Maintenance & Housing Society Rules
- **Society Charges Allocation**:
  - Regular Monthly Society Maintenance (gym, security, lift, common lights): Usually paid by tenant or included in rent.
  - Non-occupancy Charges: Maximum capped at 10% of service charges by Maharashtra Cooperative Societies (MCS) Act.
  - Sinking Fund & Major Structural Repair Funds: 100% paid by flat owner/Licensor.
- **Internal Maintenance**:
  - Owner: Structural leaks, external sewage, geyser replacement, pipeline faults.
  - Tenant: Daily fixtures, tap washers, AC cleaning (cap: ₹500–₹1,000).

---

## 5. Landlord Access & Verification Rules
- **Advance Notice**: Minimum 24 hours prior written notice before entry.
- **Permitted Entry Hours**: 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM.
- **Unannounced Inspections**: Strict prohibition against arbitrary walk-ins without prior tenant schedule confirmation.

---

## 6. Rent Escalation & Stamp Duty
- **Standard Benchmark**: 5% to 10% annual escalation upon renewal (standard 5% for 11 months; 10% for 22–33 month multi-year registered agreements).
- **Stamp Duty & Registration Fee**: Stamp duty is 0.25% of total license fee + deposit, with fixed ₹1,000 registration fee.

---

## 7. Common Predatory Clauses & Negotiation Scripts

### Predatory Clause: Unregistered Agreement / Oral License
> *"Agreement shall remain on non-judicial stamp paper without sub-registrar registration."*
- **Tenant Counter**: *"Under Section 55 of Maharashtra Rent Control Act, registration is legally mandatory. Could we complete standard biometric e-Registration via IGR Maharashtra?"*

### Predatory Clause: Unilateral License Revocation Without Notice
> *"Licensor may revoke license with 24 hours notice at their sole discretion."*
- **Tenant Counter**: *"Could we standardize this clause with mutual 30 days written notice to maintain stability for both parties?"*
